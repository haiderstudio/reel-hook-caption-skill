Topic: How I cut my editing time from 6 hours to 47 minutes

Key points:
1. I edit in the order the video will be watched: hook first, then the middle, then the ending.
2. I cut pauses and dead air before I touch colour or music.
3. I keep one caption style for the whole video, so I never re-decide it.
4. I export a draft, watch it once on my phone, and fix only what I notice.
